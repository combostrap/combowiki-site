====== How to get ComboWiki Up and Running? ======

===== About =====
We offer several ways to get ComboWiki up and running.

We are sorry, but you need to choose the best way that fits your need.


===== Next Step: Choose your installation method  =====

\\

<note width=500 align="center">
Note: We recommend [[2_docker|the Docker installation]] as:
  * it's the easiest way to get DokuWiki up and running
  * it contains all performance cache system (php-fpm and opcache)
  * a Server DokuWiki installation is not easy and straightforward
  * and [[:docs:admin:docker#features|more]] such as storing your site on Git.
</note>
